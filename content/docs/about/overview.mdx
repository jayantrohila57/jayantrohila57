Hi — I’m **Jayant Rohila**, a **product engineer** based in **Noida, India**.

## What I do

I design and ship **web products**: full-stack applications with a product-minded focus — clear UX, typed APIs, and maintainable code. My day job is **Product Engineer / Consultant–Product Engineer** at **[aiQmen](https://github.com/jayantaiqmen)** (since May 2026). Before that I spent two years at **Binmile Technologies** as an **Associate Software Developer**, building and delivering client-facing software.

Outside employment I maintain **open-source projects and experiments** on [GitHub](https://github.com/jayantrohila57) — blogging platforms, env tooling, task management, component libraries, and Next.js templates. This site documents that work in one place.

## Stack I reach for often

From public repos and recent projects, you’ll see:

- **Frontend:** TypeScript, React, Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS, Radix / shadcn-style UI
- **Backend & data:** Node.js, tRPC, Prisma, PostgreSQL (including Neon), REST where it fits
- **Auth & platform:** NextAuth / Better Auth, Vercel deployments, Docker when needed
- **Content:** TipTap, Sanity, headless CMS patterns

See **[Skills](/work/skills)** for a fuller list tied to project evidence.
