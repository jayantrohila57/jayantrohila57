## Mission

Publish a **single, consistent public identity** for Jayant Rohila — professional facts, presence links, and mention normalization — with **Apple-like consistency** across surfaces, without turning the site into a personal legal vault.

## In scope

- Employment titles, employers, and **public** dates (see [Career](/career/timeline))
- Education and **public** certifications
- Canonical links ([Presence](/presence/website))
- Open-source projects with public repos ([Projects](/work/projects))
- Cross-links to company/product docs (e.g. Lucsum) **without** implying employment

## Out of scope

- Phone numbers, home addresses, government IDs
- Salary, equity, offer letters, HR investigations, bank details
- Private email except where a future policy explicitly adds one public contact field
- Unverified gossip or scraped PII

## Source hierarchy

1. **HR / institutional records** (titles, dates) when available to the editor  
2. **Canonical URLs** in this repo ([Sources](/archive/sources))  
3. **Notion** as working draft SoT before publish  
4. Third-party mentions — normalize via [Mentions](/archive/mentions) and [Conflicts](/archive/conflicts)

## Publishing

Changes land via git, deploy through the normal pipeline, and are summarized in [Changelog](/normalize/changelog).
