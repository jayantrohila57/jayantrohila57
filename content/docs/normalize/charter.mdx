## Mission

Maintain **Apple-like consistency** for Jayant Rohila’s **public identity** across owned site, LinkedIn, GitHub, Linktree, and third-party mentions — without turning jayantrohila.com into a legal or HR document store.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Notion** — working source of truth (career, projects, ledger, research notes).  
2. **This git repo** — curated **public published slice** deployed to https://jayantrohila.com.  
3. **Primary public URLs** — LinkedIn, GitHub, certificates, deploy homepages.  
4. **Mentions ledger** — footprint URLs; resolve conflicts explicitly.

## In scope

Public titles, dates, work cities, education, certifications, repos/deploys, handles, and conflict documentation.

## Never publish

Salary, bank/HDFC/payroll, remun figures, phone numbers, employee IDs, private work email, HR/appointment/offer PDFs, personal documents, or private Drive links.

## Cross-links (not employment)

**Lucsum** and similar names — company/docs hubs only. Interviews (PeLocal, Limetray) and applications (Servoedge) are **not** career rows.

## Skills & projects

List skills **without** invented years-per-skill. Projects table must match public GitHub + deploy URLs from Notion Projects Portfolio.
