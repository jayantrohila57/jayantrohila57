Before merging a Notion export to `main`:

1. **Redact** — Strip salary, bank, phone, emp ID, private work email, HR PDF text/links.  
2. **Classify** — Employment vs interview vs hub (Lucsum).  
3. **Canonicalize** — Pick one title/date; log alternates in [Conflicts](/archive/conflicts).  
4. **Career** — Update [timeline](/career/timeline) + employer pages (incl. [Braeon](/career/braeon) if needed).  
5. **Work** — Refresh [Projects](/work/projects) repo/deploy table and [Skills](/work/skills).  
6. **Presence** — Align [Website](/presence/website), [LinkedIn](/presence/linkedin), [GitHub](/presence/github), [Linktree](/presence/linktree).  
7. **Archive** — Append or refresh [Mentions](/archive/mentions) rows (URL required).  
8. **Home** — Update `/` preview teasers in `src/components/landing.tsx`.  
9. **Changelog** — Record sync date on [Changelog](/normalize/changelog).  
10. **Build** — `pnpm build` locally.
