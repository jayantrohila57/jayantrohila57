---
title: Checklist
description: Steps to normalize a new public fact into the archive.
---

Use this checklist before merging content to `main`:

1. **Classify** — Is the fact public, professional, and free of PII/legal restrictions? If not, stop.
2. **Source** — Add or update a row in [Sources](/archive/sources) or the Notion ledger with a URL or HR reference.
3. **Canonical field** — Pick one title, date range, or URL; log alternatives in [Conflicts](/archive/conflicts).
4. **Cross-link** — Update [Timeline](/career/timeline), employer page, and [Bio](/about/bio) if needed.
5. **Presence** — Ensure [LinkedIn](/presence/linkedin), [GitHub](/presence/github), and [Linktree](/presence/linktree) agree or document divergence.
6. **Mentions** — If a third party published the fact, add ledger entry (URL required) under [Mentions](/archive/mentions).
7. **Landing** — If the fact is “current role” level, update the `/` landing hero copy.
8. **Changelog** — Note the change in [Changelog](/normalize/changelog).
9. **Build** — Run `pnpm build` locally before opening a PR.
